SHARED PRINCIPLES

What stays constant.

01

Deterministic before generative. Normal workflow logic owns routing, state and validation whenever possible.

02

Explicit state. The system tracks what happened, what is pending and what has already been processed.

03

Verification before action. Noisy external data is normalized before it can influence downstream behaviour.

04

Operational resilience. Retries, fallbacks, health checks, reconciliation and monitoring are designed in.

05

Privacy by design. Public architecture is separated from production secrets and personal data.

PORTFOLIO RULE

Proof without secrets.

The public work demonstrates architecture and engineering decisions. Production credentials, live identifiers, personal data, proprietary rules and production workflow exports stay private.
