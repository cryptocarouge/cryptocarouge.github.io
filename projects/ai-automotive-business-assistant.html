THE PROBLEM

Turn a conversational interface into a reliable operational tool.

Small business automation often mixes customer lookup, document photos, PDFs, spreadsheets and communication. The system keeps those actions explicit instead of hiding business state changes inside a chatbot prompt.

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

What matters under the hood.

✓
Multimodal, but structured
Photo input enters a dedicated vision/document path before downstream operations.
✓
Conversation is not the workflow engine
Lookup, save, PDF generation and counters remain explicit operational stages.
✓
Persist structured state
Business data is stored independently of conversational output.
✓
Keep actions inspectable
Each operation has deterministic inputs and outputs, making failures easier to diagnose.
PUBLIC / PRIVATE BOUNDARY

Proof without secrets.

The public version excludes customer records, contact details, live Sheet identifiers, webhook endpoints, credentials and the production workflow JSON.
TAKEAWAY

AI can front an operation without owning the operation.

The project shows how to combine a conversational surface with deterministic business actions so the LLM assists the workflow instead of becoming the workflow.
