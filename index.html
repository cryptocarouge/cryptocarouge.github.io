ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

Control around the model.

01

Deterministic before generative. Routing, state and validation stay in normal workflow logic where AI is unnecessary.

02

Explicit state. Systems track what happened, what is pending and what has already been processed.

03

Verification before action. External facts are normalized and checked before downstream decisions.

04

Operational resilience. Retries, fallbacks, health checks and degradation alerts are part of the design.

05

Privacy by design. Public architecture is separated from production secrets, personal data and internal endpoints.
